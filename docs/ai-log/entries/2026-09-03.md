## Session 6 · Why structure carries 0.35 — the measurement that justifies the weight

**Asked.** Re-measure the confidence separation against the shipped `structure.rs` rather than a
reference walker, and record what actually separates a real file from a decoy.

**The finding, measured through shipped code.**

Free space in the fixture carries 21 planted decoys — 8 bare JPEG headers and 13 bare GZIP
headers. Scored through `structure::validate` and `confidence::confidence`:

```
35 planted, carvable   min 0.9000   max 1.0000   mean 0.9571
21 residue decoys      min 0.5186   max 0.6500   mean 0.5805
lowest true positive 0.9000   highest false positive 0.6500   gap 0.2500, no overlap
```

Per-term weighted contribution to the mean-to-mean separation:

```
term 1  signature_integrity  w=0.40   +0.0190   <- not separation, see below
term 2  structural_validity  w=0.35   +0.3458
term 3  entropy_consistency  w=0.15   +0.0118
term 4  size_plausibility    w=0.10   +0.0000
```

**Structure supplies more than the entire gap.** 0.3458 against a gap of 0.2500. Size supplies
exactly nothing — every decoy scores full marks. Entropy supplies a tenth of what structure does.

**Signature integrity supplies zero, and this is the point.** All 8 residue JPEGs resolve a valid
`FF D9` footer in sequence, so all 8 score a perfect 1.0000 — identical to all 5 planted JPEGs.
At the signature layer a noise blob and a photograph are indistinguishable. The GZIP decoys score
0.7500, and so do the planted GZIPs, because GZIP defines no terminator and term 1 caps at the
footerless ceiling. Within every kind, signature integrity awards a decoy exactly what it awards
the real file.

Term 1's apparent +0.0190 is a kind-mix artefact and must never be quoted as separation: the
residue population is 62% footerless GZIP while the planted population is 43%. The test asserts
per-kind equality so this cannot be misread later.

**Why it matters.** This is the empirical justification for spending 0.35 on structure, and it
answers the obvious jury question — why not just match magic bytes? Because magic bytes cannot
tell a photograph from noise, and we can show the 8 cases where they do not. It belongs in
`architecture.md` and on the UI confidence panel, where the four-term stacked bar makes it visible
rather than asserted.

**One earlier claim refuted.** "All 21 decoys score 0.000 on structure" is false against shipped
code. The residue GZIP at offset 173,564,124 scores 0.2500 — its 10-byte header parses cleanly and
only the DEFLATE stream fails, so it earns real partial rubric credit. Its hard `valid` gate is
still false, as for all 21, so nothing is admitted.

**The headroom is far thinner than anyone had written down.** The admission gate is 0.75. A decoy
scoring full marks on signature, entropy and size — which all 8 residue JPEGs already do — breaches
that gate at structural credit (0.75 - 0.65) / 0.35 = 0.2857. The worst decoy today sits at 0.2500.
So the true margin is **0.0357**, not the 0.4700 previously claimed against the retired 0.90 target.
The harness tripwire was set at 0.40, above the breach point: forcing residue structure to 0.30 kept
it green while four JPEG decoys reached 0.7550 and cleared the gate. Corrected, derived from the
weights rather than hardcoded, and confirmed by mutation.

**Unsure.**
- The 0.2500 gap is measured on this fixture only. There are no PNG, PDF, ZIP, SQLite or MP4 decoys
  in it, so false-positive behaviour on those five kinds is unmeasured.
- The residue span policy for footerless decoys is a choice, not a measurement. Only the adversarial
  ceiling of 0.6500 — entropy and size pinned to 1.0 for every decoy — is safe to quote against a
  challenge to that choice.
- This measures what `confidence()` says about a correctly recovered object. It is not the recall
  claim; `bifragment.rs` has not been measured yet.

## Session 6b · Refusing to add a Txt signature row — a decision worth logging

**What happened.** The manifest labelled 5 plaintext files `signature-only`, implying they were
recoverable. Plain text has no magic bytes. Two agents, on independent paths and without being
told, arrived at the same conclusion: the honest ceiling is 33 of 40, not 38.

Both also found the shortcut. Every corpus TXT file opens with the ASCII banner
`SENTINELWIPE FIXTURE RECORD`. Adding that as a signature row would lift recall to 38 in an
afternoon, turn every test green, and cost nothing to implement.

**It was named as a contract break and refused, not quietly adopted.** That is the right call and it
is logged here as a positive decision rather than a rejection, because the reasoning is reusable:

A carver that finds text files only because we planted a marker for it to find measures nothing.
The recall number would rise while the engine got no better. Worse, it is falsifiable in one
question — *"what happens when the text file wasn't written by you?"* — and the honest answer would
have to be *"we don't find it"*, delivered after the number is already on the slide. The failure
mode of the shortcut is not that it is wrong; it is that it looks right until a jury tests it.

The alternative answer is stronger: signature carving keys on magic bytes, plain text has none, and
a content-based text carver is a real technique we have named as future work rather than faked.
That sentence demonstrates understanding of what carving is. Thirty-eight would have demonstrated
that we can pass our own exam.

**Consequence, applied.** `CLAUDE.md` and `docs/demo_script.md` now say 33 of 40 and break the seven
down as five plaintext plus two deliberately fragmented. The narrator note carries the refused
shortcut explicitly, so the answer is ready if a juror asks why the number is not higher.

**Unsure.** The 5 TXT files are still labelled `signature-only` in the generated manifest. The
reclassification to `unrecoverable-by-design` is pending a fixture rebuild, held while measurement
runs read `out/fixture.manifest.json` as ground truth.

## Session 7 · The carving deliverable — schema frozen, engine running, recall measured

**Asked.** Reordered for demo value, not engineering completeness: guard the silently-excluded
module class of bug; freeze the JSON output schema before writing the engine; build carve.rs and
the binary for contiguous objects only; measure demonstrated recall separately from the
reachability ceiling; cross-check against The Sleuth Kit; defer bifragment.

**Produced.**
- `tests/test_module_declarations.py`. Walks all five crates and refuses a module file no crate
  root declares. Verified against the original defect rather than assumed: commenting out
  `pub mod confidence;` fails the suite naming `confidence.rs`. Carries its own vacuity guard,
  because a walk that silently found nothing would make every assertion pass — the same shape as
  the bug it catches.
- Schema frozen: `docs/output_schema.md`, `fixtures/sample_output.json` (56 records, 89,773 bytes,

- `carve.rs` + the `carve` binary. 92 candidates scanned, 34 suppressed, 58 records, 33 admitted,

- Reachability ceiling: 33 of 40. Demonstrated recall: **28 of 40**, byte-exact, joined on SHA-256.

**Admitted is not recovered, and the gap is the interesting part.** 33 admitted, 28 byte-exact.

**The cross-check result.** Both tools recover 28 of 40 and they are not the same 28 — TSK-only 7,

## Session 8 · Bifragment gap carving — 2 of 5, and why the other 3 are three different problems

**Asked.** Step 4, last by the operator's ordering: complete bifragment, wire it in, re-measure
recall, margin and cost. Defer the Python wrapper with it.

**Produced.** Demonstrated recall **30 of 40** with `--reassemble`, **28 of 40** by default, both
joined on SHA-256 and both re-derived by the operator independently. `entropy_heatmap.png` and
`imaging_transcript.txt.gz` recovered byte-exact with extents equal to the manifest's element for
element. cargo 252+19+5+12, pytest 224 passed 0 skipped.

**The ≥60% fragmented bar is measured and NOT met: 2 of 5 = 40%.** The test says so in those
words. No bar was lowered, no percentage rounded. This is a build-pack acceptance criterion
failing and it goes to the SPOC as a stated limit.

**The three failures are three different kinds of problem, and only one is ours.**
- `disposal_certificate.pdf`: a validator gap. `structure::pdf` verifies 34 of 34 xref offsets
  without decoding a stream body, so a 21 kB FlateDecode payload is unread and 10 splices all
  validate. The bound is not the cause — the manifest's own splice is inside the lattice and is
  accepted. Fixable.
- `sealing_procedure.mov`: a limit of the FORMAT. `mdat` declares its own length in fragment 1, so
  any tail of the right length tiles perfectly, and QuickTime carries no checksum over sample
  data. 6,660 splices accept, one is correct, and no byte in the format separates them. No
  structure validator can fix this, ours or anyone's. This is the most jury-worthy sentence the
  project has produced.
- `handover_briefing.mov`: never searched, correctly. `structure::mp4` accepts the contiguous read
  — right length to the byte, wrong bytes — so the precondition declines. The right rule, and here
  it costs a recovery.

**A verifier fabricated an object the engine admitted at 1.0000, and that is the finding of the
session.** One real 2 KiB JPEG header prefix written onto free space produced admitted
`reassembled` records at 13 in 100 cluster-aligned offsets, all four terms 1.0000. On the frozen
fixture nothing had moved — exactly 2 of 58 records changed and the margin block was byte-identical
— so the "reassembly does not enlarge the false-positive surface" claim was true of the fixture and
false of the engine. The fixture simply contained no case able to test it.

Two rules closed most of it: determinacy now requires a real contradiction on the shrink side
rather than counting an unspliceable neighbour as a pass (every fabrication sat at the lattice
floor where three sides were being reported as four), and a second extent below one cluster is not
stated as an object. 13 -> 6 -> 2 in 100, costing no recovery. The remaining 2 are PUBLISHED, not
closed: the cause is in `structure/jpeg.rs`, and fixing it would change already-published
contiguous numbers, so it is written down rather than patched quietly.

**An earlier figure of ours was wrong by 3.2x.** Build reports quoted 0.300000 as the worst
rejected-assembly structural credit; that was the residue population with the planted files
filtered out. Measured over the plants too, `media_inventory.docx` reaches 0.957143 and
`entropy_heatmap.png` reaches 0.989286 one cluster off its true splice. Both are rejected by the
validator's hard gate today, and both are now named constants so they cannot drift unnoticed.

**One attribution corrected under challenge.** A shipped test asserted that reversal is what stops
`evidence_bag_seal.jpg`. Re-planting its true bytes FORWARD recovers 0 of 24 layouts, while PNG and
GZIP of the same shape recover 24 of 24 — so reversal is sufficient but was not shown to be
necessary, and the real cause is determinacy. The tri-fragment claim IS demonstrable and is now
demonstrated on a real 63,749-byte OOXML file: recovered at two fragments in 65 validations,
refused at three.

**Demo consequence, decided on measurement.** Reassembly costs 40x — 66.4 s against 1.65 s — for
two files. It stays OFF by default and off the demo path; the 90-second script cannot carry a
66-second carve. The spoken line already says twenty-eight, so no narration changed.

**Unsure.**
- Reassembly false-positive behaviour is measured only for GZIP, JPEG and ZIP, the kinds with
  non-contiguous candidates in this image. PNG, PDF, SQLITE and MP4 are unmeasured.
- The interleaved pair is real in the fixture but untested against it, because both are refused —
  there is nothing there to reassemble.
- `handover_briefing.mov`'s "never searched" is inferred from observable consequences, not read
  off a flag. If `structure::mp4` is later made stricter, the recorded reason becomes wrong before
  any assertion goes red.
- The Python wrapper is still not built, per the operator's instruction that it defers with
  bifragment and the Tauri frontend calls the binary directly.
