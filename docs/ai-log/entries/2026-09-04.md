# 2026-09-04

## Session 11 · The desktop shell

**Asked.** Build the Tauri shell — the frontend inbuilt, a native binary.

**Produced.** desktop/, a standalone Tauri v2 crate deliberately outside the core
workspace so the webview tree never enters the engine's lockfile. It bundles exactly the
two pages (stage.py refuses a page whose payload block is missing), exposes no IPC, and
its CSP starts at default-src 'none'. `make app` builds SENTINELWIPE.app: **4.8 MB
measured**, adhoc-signed, zero network-related libraries in `otool -L`. The pages passed a
CSP pre-flight under the exact policy before the shell was ever built, and the instrument's
wordmark now links back to the film because a native window has no browser chrome.

**What I verified, and what I could not.** The app runs stable with a 1710x1073 content
window and 398 accessibility nodes where a failed load yields a handful; compile-time
embedding hard-fails on missing dist files; the asset key /approach.html is in the binary.
What I did NOT verify: the rendered pixels. screencapture needs Screen Recording
permission, the AX value tree is closed to WKWebView without enhanced-UI mode, and two
brotli scans of the embedded assets failed — the second because brotli.decompress refuses
trailing garbage, so the scan was structurally incapable of succeeding at the true offset,
a defect in my probe I only saw after reading its suspicious 2-second runtime. The window
is open on the operator's screen; the operator is the final verifier.

**A smaller lesson repeated.** My first refresh-pipeline runner treated the post-wipe
carve's exit 1 as failure. The engine says it itself: "no candidate reached the gate.
exit 1 — a complete report, not an error." On the post-wipe pass, admitting zero is the
result the demo exists to produce.
